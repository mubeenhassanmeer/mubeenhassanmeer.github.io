01 / 03 Autonomous Mobile Robot · Final Year Project

The Warehouse Learns to Walk

A 250 kg-capacity, 6-way shuttle AMR built to move inventory the way the best automated warehouses in the world already do — studied from systems like Zikoo Robotics and Mecalux, then reverse-engineered and rebuilt from first principles for our own floor. It also happens to be my Final Year Project, which means the stakes are real on two scorecards at once.

  • Team Lead — electronics, embedded control, navigation, debugging
  • Reports directly to the CEO for fund approvals & sourcing

02 / 03 Reverse-Engineered Inventory Automation

Breaking It Down to Build It Better

An imported inventory system from Monolith arrived as a sealed black box. It's leaving as an STM32-powered, RS485-networked, RFID-secured system I designed after months of teardown, direct supplier negotiation in China, and a full architecture redesign — now deep in PCB layout.
